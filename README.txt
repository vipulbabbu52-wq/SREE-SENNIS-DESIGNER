SREE SENNIS DESIGNER - Billing website

HOW TO PUT IT ON NETLIFY
1. Unzip this folder on your computer.
2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign in (free).
3. Drag the whole unzipped folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a web address.
4. Open that address. In the app go to Settings, type that address into
   'Your website address', and tap Save settings.
5. Now, on any bill, tap 'Send on WhatsApp'. The customer link works until
   30 days after the delivery date.

IMPORTANT
- Bills are stored in the browser of the device you use. Download a backup
  from the Backup page often.
- Anyone who has a customer link can open that bill until it expires.
